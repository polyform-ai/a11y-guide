# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in this project to be respectful, welcoming, and accessible. Be considerate, assume good intent, give specific and constructive feedback, and make room for people with different backgrounds and access needs.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that conflict with this standard.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
